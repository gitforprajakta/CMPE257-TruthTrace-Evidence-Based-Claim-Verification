Methods

Both models are logistic regression. They differ in how claim and evidence become features. The tuning budget is the same: the same inverse-regularization grid, the same stratified folds, and the same macro-F1 selection rule.

TF-IDF + logistic regression

Stage 1 model. Word TF-IDF on the claim, on the evidence, and a numeric flag for whether any evidence was present. Claim-only and evidence-only ablations drop the other text.

Sentence-BERT + logistic regression

Stage 2 model. A sentence encoder embeds the claim and the evidence. Evidence is embedded per question-answer pair and averaged. The classifier sees the claim vector, the evidence vector, their absolute difference, and their product, plus the evidence flag in the full setting.

Evaluation protocol

  • Train on the official AVeriTeC train split. Evaluate once on the official dev split.
  • Stratified cross-validation uses 5 folds, shuffled with seed 42.
  • The shared inverse-regularization grid is 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100.
  • TF-IDF also chooses a word or word-plus-bigram range by the same cross-validated macro-F1.
  • Class weights are balanced. The selection metric is macro-F1.
  • Test-set macro-F1 is reported with a percentile bootstrap interval of 1000 resamples.
  • Stage 2 compares the full models with McNemar's test and a paired bootstrap of the macro-F1 difference.
  • A majority-class classifier is the baseline. It uses the full split only.

The encoder named in the plan for Stage 2 is sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2.
